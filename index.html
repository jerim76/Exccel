Tuition built around the learner.

Excel Tuition Centre provides focused one-to-one online tuition for learners following local and international curricula.

Every learner is different. Some need more time with difficult topics. Others need examination practice, confidence or a more structured approach to revision.

Our virtual lessons allow the tutor to work directly with one learner at a time, identify areas that need attention and adapt the lesson accordingly.

Are the lessons one-to-one? +

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre provides live one-to-one online tuition. Each lesson is focused on one learner and one tutor.

Where can learners join from? +

Learners can join from Kenya or from anywhere in the world with a suitable internet connection.

Do you support homeschooling? +

Yes. We can provide structured one-to-one academic support for families using homeschooling or flexible learning arrangements.

Which curricula do you support? +

We support a range of international curricula including Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, International Baccalaureate, AQA, OCR and WJEC/Eduqas.

Do you prepare students for SAT and IELTS? +

Yes. We provide targeted one-to-one preparation for SAT and IELTS, alongside other international examinations.

Can I request a subject that is not listed? +

Yes. Contact us with the learner's subject, curriculum, level and goals and we can discuss the available support.
